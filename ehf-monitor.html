    Headphone Accommodations off (Settings › Accessibility › Audio & Visual). It reshapes high frequencies and will silently move your thresholds.
  • ·
    Same noise-control mode and same seal. Reseat the buds until they feel identical. Insertion depth alone can move 14 kHz by 10 dB or more.

Why this doesn't tell you whether you have hearing loss

Thresholds here are in dBFS — how far below your device's maximum output the tone sits. That is not dB HL. Turning dBFS into dB HL needs a transducer calibrated against a reference standard, and above 8 kHz those reference values don't exist for consumer earbuds at all.

What this can do is detect change in you, measured on the same hardware, under the same conditions, against your own baseline. That's the useful question anyway.

A threshold that shifts by a few dB between sessions is normal measurement scatter, mostly from earbud seal. Treat 5 dB as noise. Treat a 15 dB shift that repeats across two separate sessions as worth a clinic visit.

Baselines

A baseline is the reference your spot checks are compared against. Set it from a threshold run on a good day, in a quiet room, with a fresh seal.

Safety ceiling
